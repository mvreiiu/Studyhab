01 | Brainstorming

How did StudyHab find its final topic and identify their target audience?

We started with discussing if the content and topic we wanted to do would work well with our target audience which are the transferee students since they are new to the environment of the Apec Schools system and we wanted to focus more on showing different ways and tips to manage their time successfully when facing multiple subject deadlines.

After considering and discussing several ideas for our content, we eventually came up with three possible themes: gaming, food, and balancing student life. Each theme was designed to accommodate the different interests, experiences, and needs of students within APEC.

Gaming

The gaming-focused content would primarily accommodate students who are interested in or actively involved in the E-Games Club, giving them an opportunity to engage with content related to their hobbies and interests.

Food

The food theme would focus on the different food preferences and experiences of APEC students.

Balancing Student Life

The student-life theme would highlight the challenges and experiences of managing academics, extracurricular activities, social life, personal responsibilities, and specifically tight deadlines.

Through these themes, we aim to create content that is relatable, engaging, and inclusive of the diverse student community in APEC.

At first, we wanted to settle with the food content since we knew that many learners tend to eat meals outside of school before or after their practices, as most of them prefer buying lunch rather than bringing food from home. We mainly wanted to highlight the local businesses near the campus that offer affordable yet enjoyable meals that cater to different tastes, instead of focusing only on popular fast-food chains such as Jollibee, KFC, McDonald’s, and many others.

Although this content provided a strong and relatable hook for our target audience, we had some hesitations about continuing with it because we recognized that a large portion of our high school learners would still prefer visiting familiar fast-food establishments rather than locally owned businesses.

Because of this, we decided to pursue a content theme centered on how to manage the time of an Apec transferee successfully when facing multiple school deadlines , which we believe would be more relevant to the everyday experiences of students. Through this content, we aim to present realistic and relatable situations that students experience while encouraging them to find a healthy balance between their responsibilities and social lives.

02 | Planning

During the planning stage, our group organized the tasks needed to develop our student life content.

Task Assignment

We first assigned each group member to a specific task so that parts of our IPBA would be distributed among the group and each part of the IPBA would have someone responsible for completing it.

Topic Questions

We then discussed possible questions related to our topic and content. These questions helped us understand the experiences and challenges that transferee students may encounter when trying to balance their time between academic and personal when facing multiple school deadlines.

Survey Planning

Instead of relying only on our own experiences as group members, we plan to do a survey around the APEC SCHOOLS - Bacoor campus to also gather responses from other transferees at different grade levels and sections.

Livestream Podcast

We planned a stream that we will be conducting on Tiktok live and the contents of our live stream will be a podcast about sharing our own experiences as a transferees and giving tips and different ways on how to manage time when there are multiple upcoming deadlines while also making space for personal time.

Website Planning

At the same time, the group planned the development of the webpage. We agreed to work together on the website and organize the collected information in a way that would be easy for viewers to understand.

Presentation Planning

We also planned the presentation that would also be related to our main topic, content, and website and assigned different members to work on the presentation slides.

Overall, the planning stage helped us organize our ideas, divide the different tasks among each member, and establish the content that would be needed for the final webpage and presentation.

03 | Survey

What type of Survey did StudyHab make and give out?

For the survey part, we made a form and gave access to SHS and JHS students.

Question 1

How does student life content help students understand the importance of balancing academics, personal life, and responsibilities?

Student life content helps students understand the balance by showing them real strategies and relatable examples for managing academics, personal time, and responsibilities together. It normalizes prioritizing tasks, setting boundaries, and avoiding burnout so students see that balance is essential, not optional.

Question 2

In what ways can student life content motivate students to improve their study habits and manage their time effectively?

Student life content can motivate students to improve their study habits and time management by sharing relatable success stories and practical, small step techniques like time blocking, the Pomodoro method, and prioritization frameworks that feel achievable rather than overwhelming.

06 | Streaming

What is StudyHab’s Streaming Topic/Content?

How APEC Transferee’s Manage Their Time When Facing Multiple School Deadlines

Our streaming topic is about how APEC transferee’s manage their time when they have multiple school work deadlines. We want to show how students handle assignments, projects, and exams that need to be finished at a specific date set by the teachers.

What We Want to Show

Our content will also show the challenges students face when trying to finish their schoolwork while still having time for themselves.

Podcast Stream

We plan to go on a livestream and do a podcast and talk about how students organize their tasks and daily activities. Through our podcast stream, we hope to share experiences and ideas that other students can relate to.

07 | Target Audience

What is StudyHab’s Target Audience?

Our target audience is mainly APEC transferee’s who often have multiple school tasks to finish. This includes students who struggle with managing their time when different deadlines are coming up. We also want to reach students who find it hard to balance their schoolwork with their hobbies and personal time.

Our content is for students who want to learn how others organize their tasks and deal with busy school days. We hope that our podcast stream will be relatable and helpful to students who experience the same thing.

08 | Viewer Needs

What are the viewer needs that StudyHab needs to meet?

1. What problem or need does your audience have?

The problem or need our group's audience have is the daily struggles of life as a student even outside of school, things like mental health, physical health, and family problems.

2. Why would they watch your content?

They can relate to our content because they are also students and if they watch our videos they can learn something from it, take advice, and receive guidance from us.

3. What value does your content provide?

Our content provides students with practical and relatable skills that help them manage their academic responsibilities while still making time for their own personal lives and having fun of their own accord.

Managing Several Tasks

Students may need help managing their time when they have several assignments and projects to complete. Some students struggle to decide which task they should do first, especially when deadlines are close together.

Relatable Guidance

They might watch our content to know about how other students handle their school responsibilities. Our podcast can give them simple ideas on how to organize their tasks and use their time wisely.

We also want our viewers to understand that they can manage their schoolwork while still making time for themselves to rest. By promoting this balanced relationship with school and social life, it would help students work better, reduce unnecessary stress, avoid burnout, and maintain their well-being while achieving their goals.
